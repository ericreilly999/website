privacy

What this site collects.

Short version: nothing goes to Google Analytics unless you accept, and you can change your mind at any time.

Analytics 01 / 06

If you accept analytics, this site loads Google Analytics 4. It records the pages you view, clicks on the Work with me buttons on the home page, whether a contact form was sent (not what it said), which version of the home page headline you saw, and standard device details: browser, screen size, the page that sent you here, and an approximate location (country and city) that Google derives from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. Google signals and ad personalization are off. Google processes this data for me. Event data is kept for 2 months. Nothing is sent to Google Analytics until you click Accept, and nothing is sent if you decline.

Your choice 02 / 06

Use privacy choices at the bottom of any page to Accept or Decline. Declining after accepting stops collection straight away and deletes the analytics cookies this site set. You can also block analytics in your browser.

Stored in your browser 03 / 06

This site keeps three small values in your browser's local storage: theme (light or dark), heroExperiment (which home page headline you see), and analyticsConsent (your analytics choice). They are not sent anywhere by this site. The headline version is attached to analytics events only if you accept analytics.

Contact form 04 / 06

When you send the contact form, your name, email, company and message go to my contact service on Amazon Web Services and are emailed to me. I use them only to reply to you.

Fonts 05 / 06

Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details when it serves them.

Questions 06 / 06

Ask me through the contact page.
